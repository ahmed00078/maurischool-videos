# MauriSchool — voice script (30 s)

**Language:** Arabic. Hassaniya is welcome. Adapt each line into natural spoken Hassaniya rather than reading the Standard Arabic word for word, but keep the meaning and keep it short.
**Tone:** calm, warm, confident. You are a trusted person talking to a school director, not an advertiser. No shouting, no "radio voice".
**Pace:** unhurried. Each line has a time window. Finishing early is better than rushing, and the video is retimed to your voice.

> Pronounce the brand as **موري سكول** (Mauri-School), and WhatsApp as **واتساب**.

## Direction, line by line

1. **A real question**, asked to one director. Stress **كم تلميذًا** ("how many pupils"). Let the question hang for half a second at the end.
2. **Slightly tired, the voice of everyday frustration.** A short pause after each item (دفاتر… إكسل… واتساب…). Lower the voice on **ضائعة** ("lost").
3. **The turn: the voice lifts and relaxes, like good news.** Stress **في تطبيق واحد** ("in one app").
4. **The key line. Clear and confident.** Take a small breath at the "…". Stress **تلقائيًا** ("automatically").
5. **Two short beats**: a tap, then a result. A brief pause between the two halves.
6. **Warm, thinking of the parent.** Stress **هاتف الوليّ** ("the parent's phone").
7. **Simple and reassuring, almost matter-of-fact.**
8. **An invitation, not an order.** Smile while saying it. Slow down on **مجانيًا** ("free").

Do not read the phone number aloud. It stays on screen.

## How to record (a good phone is enough)

- **Quiet, soft room:** a bedroom with curtains, cushions or clothes around, not a tiled room. Turn off fans and the AC, and put phones on silent.
- **Phone about 20 cm from the mouth, slightly to the side,** so "p" and "b" sounds don't pop. Use the phone's voice memo app at its best quality setting.
- **Record each line separately, 3 takes each.** Leave 2 seconds of silence before and after every take.
- **Say the line number before each take,** for example "واحد، تجربة ثانية" ("one, second take"). I'll cut it out.
- **Keep the same distance and energy for all lines** so they sound like one continuous voice.
- **Send the original files** (m4a or wav) by email or as *documents* on WhatsApp, not as voice notes. Voice notes are heavily compressed.

## After recording

Send the files. The video is retimed to the voice, and the Arabic captions are updated to match exactly what is said, including any Hassaniya wording. The French captions stay as they are.
